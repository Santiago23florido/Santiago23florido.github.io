About

I trained as a mechatronics engineer in Colombia, where I worked on the modelling and digital control of physical systems, and then moved to Paris to study computer science and artificial intelligence at ENSTA Paris, alongside a master's in Cyber-Physical Systems at Institut Polytechnique de Paris. In between I spent a summer at Inria on the question of when a neural network should grow rather than keep training.

What I do now sits between reinforcement learning in simulation and the decoding of biological signals. Both halves ask the same question from opposite ends: what has to be true about a skill, or about an intention, for it to still mean something once the body carrying it out is not the one it came from. On one side that means moving a locomotion policy between humanoids of very different scale; on the other, reading individual motor units out of a muscle signal while the limb is moving, so that a hand can be driven from what a person intended rather than from what a sensor happened to measure.
