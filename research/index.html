Research

What I work on, and the papers that have come out of it.

Interests

Cross-embodiment transfer

Moving a learned locomotion skill between humanoids of very different scale, in simulation. The interesting part is not the policy but the interface: what a skill has to be expressed in so that a body it was never trained on can execute it.

Neuromotor interfaces

Decoding individual motor units from high-density surface EMG while the limb is moving — which is exactly when existing methods lose track of them, and exactly when an interface has to stay usable.

Dexterous manipulation

Musculoskeletal and robotic hands, teleoperation, and the context a muscle signal cannot carry on its own: two different intentions can produce almost the same activation, and what separates them lies in the situation.

Safe and efficient learning

Constrained MDPs where a safety budget is stated in units someone can argue with, physics-structured dynamics models, and networks that add capacity only once they can certify they need it.

Low-order dynamic modeling and control of a neonatal incubator

Santiago Florido Gómez, José David Gómez Bedoya, Tatiana Manrique, Róbinson A. Torres

2025 IEEE 7th Colombian Conference on Automatic Control (CCAC)

A thermodynamic model of a commercial incubator reduced to a control-friendly low-order form by system identification, with discrete-time PID and incremental RST controllers that outperform the stock controller.
